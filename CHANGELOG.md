# Shockwave 1.1.0

You can now delete songs from inside the app.

Right-click a track or use the trash icon on hover, confirm, and Shockwave moves the audio file to the Recycle Bin instead of erasing it permanently. The song is removed from your library and playlists. Restore it from the Recycle Bin if you change your mind.
